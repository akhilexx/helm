Why Helm exists

Most trading venues treat the oracle as a number that always arrives. Helm treats it as a claim that can be late, wide, or jumped — and refuses to mark, liquidate or resolve when that claim fails its checks.

Perps

Isolated-margin SOL, BTC and XAU perpetuals. Every open, increase, withdraw and liquidation is gated by oracle age, confidence and jump bounds. A jumped print flips the market to reduce-only instead of becoming the new mark.

Forecast

Binary price-threshold markets. Trading uses the live Predict pm-AMM on Solana devnet (mock USDC), not a homemade curve. Predict’s own docs say resolution is still a manual authority call with no oracle or dispute process. Helm supplies that missing piece: a Pyth Hermes threshold, the same oracle checks as the perps engine, a dispute window, and a void if the oracle never becomes safe. Helm will not call resolveMarket on a bad print.

What this is not

This is a hackathon demonstration on Solana devnet. It is not available for real-money use. It is not wired to mainnet USDC even though the pm-AMM program ID is shared across clusters.
