Why Helm

A risk rule, not another tape that always believes the last print.

01

Pyth oracle safety

Every open, increase, withdraw and liquidation is gated by oracle age, confidence width, and jump bounds. A late, wide, or jumped print is treated as a failed claim, not a new mark.

02

On-chain risk engine

Isolated-margin perps with health as equity over maintenance margin. Default demo parameters: 10% initial margin, 5% maintenance, 10× leverage cap, 4096-slot max age, 2% max confidence, 5% max jump.

03

Forecast on the pm-AMM

Binary threshold markets trade on the live Predict pm-AMM on Solana devnet (mock USDC), not a homemade curve. Helm proposes YES or NO only from a safe Pyth print, waits out a dispute window, and voids the market rather than guessing.

How it works

From a Pyth print to a position — or a halt.

01

A keeper stamps the tape

Real Pyth prices are written to each market account. The web app reads those accounts live and does not invent a mark when an account is missing.

02

You trade isolated margin

Deposit devnet SOL as collateral, then open or increase a long or short on SOL-PERP, BTC-PERP, or XAU-PERP. Liquidation price uses the same math the unit tests cover.

03

Unsafe prints trip the circuit

If age, confidence, or jump checks fail, the market goes reduce-only. New risk is blocked; reduces still work; liquidation still requires a live oracle.

04

Forecasts resolve — or they don’t

After expiry, Helm checks Pyth the same way. A safe print proposes an outcome and opens a dispute window. A later disagreeing safe print discards the proposal. A persistently unsafe oracle is halted, then treated as void.

FAQ

Straight answers from the repo.

What is Helm?+

Helm is an isolated-margin perpetual-futures venue on Solana devnet, plus a forecast (prediction-market) resolver. It refuses to mark, trade, liquidate, or resolve on a stale, wide, or jumped oracle.

Which markets are live?+

Three perpetual markets ship in the demo: SOL-PERP, BTC-PERP, and XAU-PERP (a gold hedge market). Collateral is native devnet SOL held in a program vault. Forecast trading uses the Predict pm-AMM with mock USDC; a seeded market is named HELM SOL >= 150.

How does the oracle guard work?+

The program checks publish age, confidence width, and jump size against per-market caps. The keeper posts real Pyth Core Compatible shard-0 prices on devnet (Hermes is used as an off-chain reference when available). The UI never fabricates a mark.

Is this real-money trading?+

No. This is a developer demonstration on Solana devnet, not available for real-money use. It is not wired to mainnet USDC even though the pm-AMM program ID is shared across clusters. Do not deposit mainnet assets.

Where is the program, and what was tested?+

The Helm program on devnet is 6edxEyrKXKAYSHcYy1wVEzF7miJ3dvYoR93TkrxfhXnd. The helm-math crate has 29 unit tests (15 perps + 14 forecast resolver) and crates/helm-math-js has 6 Node tests, all recorded passing on 10 Nov 2026 (Asia/Calcutta).
